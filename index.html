After importing a PDF, scroll in the text box above and tap a word to place the cursor. Tap “Read from cursor” to skip straight there. Selecting text starts at the first selected word. The blue button starts at your cursor and pauses while reading. Start / Resume continues your reading position; Restart goes back to the beginning.

📝 Reading Notes

One continuous lined sheet. Choose plain text, dot bullets, or an outline: I. → A. → 1. → a. → i. Use Indent / Outdent to change levels; Enter continues the list and an empty item ends it. Notes save in this browser; Save reading + notes makes a portable copy.

Draw with your finger or Apple Pencil. Scroll outside the sheet to move down. Add more paper when you need it; Save PNG exports the whole handwritten sheet.
